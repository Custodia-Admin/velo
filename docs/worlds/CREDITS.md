# World credits

Velo's baked worlds (`public/worlds/<id>/`) are built only from open data by
`tools/worldbake/bake.mjs`. Nothing here needs an API key, at bake time or at
ride time. Each source, its licence and what we owe it are listed below,
followed by the exact tiles, bounding box and OSM snapshot each world used
(generated by the bake; don't edit those blocks by hand).

The app code is MIT. The world bundles are data and carry the licences below.
The in-app credits line comes from each bundle's `manifest.json` `credits`.
The repository-wide notice (upstream GlobeRide credit, data, optional
services, trademarks) is [NOTICE.md](../../NOTICE.md).

### USGS 3D Elevation Program (3DEP) 1/3 arc-second DEM (bare ground; Cuyahoga Valley, Cleveland, Santa Monica)

- Used for: the heights within 450 m of the routes in worlds whose recipe
  sets `"bareGround": { "source": "usgs-3dep-13", … }` (see
  [WORLD-FORMAT.md](WORLD-FORMAT.md) "Bare ground"): the routes, and the
  ground, trees and buildings beside them, stand on the bare earth rather
  than on GLO-30's canopy. The bundle carries the `usgs-3dep` credit.
- URL template (pinned dated releases, which never change):
  `https://prd-tnm.s3.amazonaws.com/StagedProducts/Elevation/13/TIFF/historical/<tile>/USGS_13_<tile>_<release>.tif`.
- Licence: public domain (a work of the U.S. Geological Survey). USGS asks
  for credit: "U.S. Geological Survey, 3D Elevation Program 1/3 arc-second
  Digital Elevation Model" (https://www.usgs.gov/information-policies-and-instructions/crediting-usgs).
- Modifications: shifted onto GLO-30's level (the median difference over
  flat open ground, −0.2 m, −0.09 m and +0.44 m), sampled bilinearly onto the world
  grid, blended into GLO-30, quantised, and re-graded along the routes.

### swissALTI3D (bare ground; Lugano)

- Used for: the heights within 450 m of the routes in worlds whose recipe

  carries the `swissalti3d` credit.

  `https://data.geo.admin.ch/ch.swisstopo.swissalti3d/swissalti3d_<year>_<E>-<N>/swissalti3d_<year>_<E>-<N>_2_2056_5728.tif`,
  releases looked up in the STAC catalogue
  `https://data.geo.admin.ch/api/stac/v0.9/collections/ch.swisstopo.swissalti3d/items`.
- Licence: Open Government Data of the Swiss Confederation (swisstopo terms
  of use, OGD since 1 March 2021): free use, including commercial, with the
  source named: "Source: Federal Office of Topography swisstopo".
- Modifications: averaged over 7 × 7 pixels (14 m) at each 15 m node,
  shifted onto GLO-30's level (−0.4 m), blended into GLO-30, quantised, and
  re-graded along the routes.

### OSRM bike routing on routing.openstreetmap.de (bake time only)

- Used for: the scenic routes' geometry, computed once by
  `scripts/build-scenic-routes.mjs` and stored in `src/lib/scenicRouteData.ts`.
  The app never calls a routing server.
- Service: FOSSGIS e.V., https://routing.openstreetmap.de/about.html. It's a

  own OSRM or Valhalla.
- The output is derived from OpenStreetMap: ODbL 1.0, **© OpenStreetMap
  contributors**.

## Services the app calls at ride time

These are not baked into any bundle; the app calls them from the rider's
browser.

- **Open-Meteo forecast API** (`https://api.open-meteo.com/v1/forecast`):
  live weather at each route's start (`src/lib/meteo/`). Free, keyless, no
  account. Data under **CC BY 4.0**; attribution: weather data by
  Open-Meteo.com.
- **OpenStreetMap standard tiles** (`https://tile.openstreetmap.org/`): the
  map under routes that no baked world covers, when no Cesium ion token is set
  (`src/lib/cesiumUtils.ts`). **© OpenStreetMap contributors**, used under the
  OSM Foundation tile usage policy
  (https://operations.osmfoundation.org/policies/tiles/).
- **Cesium ion** (imagery, World Terrain and Google Photorealistic 3D Tiles):
  only with the rider's own token. Their terms apply.
- **Remote world bundles**: published to a public Cloudflare R2 bucket
  (`tools/worldbake/publish.mjs`) and fetched on the first ride of that world.
  Same data licences as above.
