## What was built (branch `velo/play-layer-t_baf67026`)

- **Ride clock.** Samples now carry `rideMs` (the ride clock at that point,
  pauses excluded). Older samples get it rebuilt from the wall clock, with
  gaps over 2 s taken out as pauses. The recorder also closes the saved track
  at the ride's end, so a ghost reaches the finish.
- **Ghost replay** (`lib/play/ghostReplay.ts`). Distance and position are
  taken from the samples on the ride clock. Each sample's lat/lon is
  projected back onto the route near its recorded distance. Test: a 4 km
  ride recorded through the real `rideStore.tick` (a 90 s stop, a stretch
  of 160 ms frames) replays within 1 m of every sample and every frame.
  `renderers/three/coupleGhost.test.ts` repeats this on the Perugia bundle
  through the renderer's driver.
- **Couple ghost.**
  - `lib/play/coupleGhost.ts` picks the other rider's last ride on this
    route, or their best complete one (≥ 98 % of the route). It matches by
    route id, or by name for older records.
  - `renderers/three/CoupleGhost.tsx` draws them as the GLB rider in their
    colours. It is translucent (55 %), fades to 18 % when level with you,
    and fades out after their ride ends. The Ghost riders toggle hides it.
  - Setup step 3 is renamed "Who rides with you". It offers the ghost as
    last ride, best ride or no ghost, above the pace partners
    (`components/velo/CoupleGhostPicker.tsx`). It defaults to the last ride
    when there is one; with no ride there is a one-line hint.
  - Finish shows a line such as "1:41 ahead of Anna's last ride (3 Nov)".
- **Postcards on the world renderer.**
  - `lib/play/threePlayLayer.ts` gives one postcard per named place
    (manifest POI) the route passes. The Perugia and Lugano routes have 5 to
    7 each.
  - In the world, a postcard is the place's own plain pin, in `--primary`
    until it is collected (`renderers/three/Landmarks.tsx`). There is no
    text in the world.
  - The Play panel shows "Postcard from <place>" when you collect one.
    Finish shows "7 of 7 postcards found · <places>".
  - On phones the panel shows only the count between pickups, which keeps
    the ride UI within its 15 %.
  - **Too few places: spaced postcards** (`worldPostcards`, card
    t_99351c07). A route that passes fewer than `POSTCARD_MIN` (3) named
    places gets photoreal's evenly spaced postcards (`placePostcards`, one
    every ~800 m) instead, never a mix. Chengdu is the case today: its only
    OSM places are 街道 districts and a peak, none within reach of either
    loop, so without this Play mode showed nothing there. A spaced postcard
    has no place and no pin, so nothing changes in the world (no floating
    pickups, per the ride-view directives). The Play panel counts down
    "Next one in 400 m" and notes "Postcard 3 collected", and Finish shows
    "3 of 14 postcards found" without names: the same copy as on photoreal.
    `threePlayLayer.test.ts` pins the rule on every catalog world, the
    boundary, and the Chengdu loops.
- **No gameplay on the trainer path.** `lib/play/playIsolation.test.ts`
  scans the play files: none imports `ftms`, `controllers`, `useRideLoop`
  or `virtualGears`, and none writes the ride store. The layer reads the
  renderer's `RideFrame` only.
